## Limits of this result

- The infeasibility proof covers the reduced averaged model (buffer inductor neglected, lossless buffer branch, 100 µs sampling). The time-domain runs test the richer averaged model but do not prove anything about it.
- No switched-model, dead-time or hardware evidence yet. AC current loops are ideal.
- One topology (the illustrative candidate of plan section 5) and one parameter set. Whether the gap survives at practical ratings across the design space is still open.
