# Switched-model check of the equal-energy pair

Generated by `sim/run_switched_check.py` from `switched_check.json`. Nothing here has been measured on hardware.

## Switched model

- Three half-bridge legs, one symmetric carrier at 20 kHz, dead time 1 µs (0 and 2 µs as variants). During dead time the freewheeling diode chosen by the pole-current sign sets the pole voltage.
- Devices: on-resistance 50 mΩ, diode drop 0.8 V.
- Control runs once per period on sampled states with a 1-period delay. The AC current loops are real (1.5 kHz crossover); duties include dead-time compensation.
- The circuit is integrated exactly between switching edges. Cutting the step limit from 5 µs to 1.25 µs moved the bus extremes by at most 0.073 V.
- Both models are judged on period-averaged bus voltage and pole currents. Instantaneous values, which include switching ripple, are listed separately.

## Limits of this check

- Ideal gate timing, no switching transients, no parasitic capacitance, and no common-mode path from the floating AC sources. Sensors and ADC quantisation are ideal.
- DC1 and DC2 remain ideal power ports; only the three-leg converter is switched.
- Same single parameter set and illustrative topology as the averaged study.
