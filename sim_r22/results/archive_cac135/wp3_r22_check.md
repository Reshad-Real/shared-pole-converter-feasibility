## Reading

- The power-flow physics of the shared-leg decoupling converter is reproduced by both models within the measurement uncertainty: the DC ripple with and without decoupling, the capacitor voltage that absorbs the double-line power (amplitude and phase), the three branch currents, and how long each modulation clamps S_a.
- THD is not reproduced: both models give a cleaner grid current than measured, even with longer dead time. R22's own PLECS simulation (Fig. 5c) also shows 2–4 % with an ideal grid, so the measured THD is set mainly by details of R22's controller that are not published. THD is therefore not a usable anchor here.
- Losses and efficiency (Table I) were excluded in advance; see `BENCHMARK.md`.
- The reproduction relies on assumed controller gains, dead time and device data (`MODEL_SPEC.md`); the waveform-level results are insensitive to them, the THD is not.
